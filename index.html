About Campus Scoop

Campus Scoop is a student-focused ice cream and dessert shop built to give you affordable treats and a comfortable, café-style place to relax and hang out with friends.

Near most colleges, dessert spots are either too pricey or too cramped to sit in for long. We wanted a place where a scoop doesn't wreck your monthly budget, where you can build your dessert exactly how you like it, and where nobody rushes you out of your seat after class.

Come alone, come with the whole group project team. Either way, there's a chair with your name on it.
